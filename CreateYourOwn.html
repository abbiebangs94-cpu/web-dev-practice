    Empire Website

    I like this show because it has great characters and a compelling plot.

    It's a black show

    The show is based on a family trying to get out the street, which they did. And now they're fighting each other to stay in power.

    My Favorite character is Cookie

    Cookie is a strong and determined character who always fights for her family and her business. She is a great role model for women and shows that you can be both tough and compassionate.
